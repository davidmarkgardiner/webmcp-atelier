# Competition strategy

## Decision

Build three internal candidates and submit only the highest-scoring one. The official rules limit an entrant to one submission, and the deadline is 3 September 2026 at 1 p.m. Pacific Time.

## Editorial correction to the model shortlist

The DeepSeek Harness team ranked a premium site-as-agent commerce concept first. That direction is intentionally not used as a candidate because the Google reference suite already contains premium coffee, sports retail, leather goods, order tracking, ticketing, and other commerce demonstrations. A plain search-cart-checkout experience would be polished but insufficiently novel.

The accepted portfolio keeps the team's strongest principles—visible state, explicit consent, deterministic data, reusable merchant patterns—while moving into three distinct product categories:

1. agent trust and supervision;
2. human-agent creative work;
3. multi-surface community coordination.

## Shared product contract

- Native imperative WebMCP tools with precise JSON Schema inputs.
- Declarative WebMCP forms where a normal accessible form is the better interface.
- Read-only and untrusted-content annotations where applicable.
- Abort-aware execution and deterministic structured results.
- A visible ledger showing tool name, arguments summary, state transition, result, and whether human approval was required.
- Preview and commit are distinct tools; destructive or consequential actions are simulated only.
- Every app works as a coherent human UI when site tools are unavailable.
- The demo path is deterministic, keyboard accessible, and completes in under three minutes.

## Evaluation

Score each candidate from 1 to 5 on the four equally weighted official dimensions: WebMCP leverage, execution, potential impact, and creativity and ambition. Add hard pass/fail gates for native ChatGPT browser discovery, deterministic demo completion, accessibility smoke checks, no secrets or live side effects, and a public repository-ready license and README.

The selected winner must have no score below 4, pass every hard gate, and present one memorable human-agent moment within the first 30 seconds of the video.
