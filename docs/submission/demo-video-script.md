# GatherGraph demo video

- Target duration: **2 minutes 25 seconds**
- Hard limit: **under 3 minutes**
- Format: public YouTube video with spoken audio; no unlicensed music or third-party footage

## Narrative

### 0:00–0:18 — The problem

**Picture:** GatherGraph hero and the example request.

**Voice:** “Planning one local event means reconciling a venue, food, and delivery across separate websites. A normal browser agent has to guess what every label and button means. GatherGraph lets each provider expose a structured contract instead.”

### 0:18–0:38 — Why WebMCP matters

**Picture:** Side-by-side comparison, then the three provider plan card.

**Voice:** “Venue, food, and logistics are independent same-origin documents. Each owns three WebMCP tools. GatherGraph owns four more tools for composition, repair, approval, and the final simulated dossier.”

**On-screen proof:** Briefly show native mode and thirteen discovered tools.

### 0:38–1:05 — One human request

**Picture:** Run the example: “Plan an accessible dinner for 40 people under £1,800.”

**Voice:** “The person describes the outcome, not the clicks. The agent gathers structured capacity, accessibility, menu, allergen, route, timing, and footprint evidence from the right provider.”

**On-screen proof:** Provider strips update; receipts appear.

### 1:05–1:30 — Visible conflict and repair

**Picture:** Workspace showing “Delivery 16:45 conflicts”; invoke repair.

**Voice:** “The first plan is not silently presented as correct. GatherGraph exposes a delivery-before-access conflict. The repair tool moves delivery to 17:15 and records exactly what changed.”

**On-screen proof:** Conflict changes to “Delivery repaired to 17:15”; ledger receipt appears.

### 1:30–1:58 — Human approval boundary

**Picture:** Request approval, show the dialog, approve, then commit the simulated dossier.

**Voice:** “Repair is not approval, and approval is not commitment. The application stops for a person. Without that receipt, dossier creation fails closed. With approval, it assembles a browser-local dossier—no booking, payment, or message is sent.”

### 1:58–2:18 — Shared control and evidence

**Picture:** Budget field, provider surfaces, execution ledger.

**Voice:** “The person and agent use the same visible plan. The human can change constraints or take over manually, while every agent action leaves durable evidence.”

### 2:18–2:25 — Close

**Picture:** Hero title and GatherGraph wordmark.

**Voice:** “GatherGraph: one gathering, three local teams, and a human who stays in control.”

## Recording checklist

- Record the deployed production build, not the development fallback unless explicitly explaining it.
- Keep browser zoom at 100% and notifications hidden.
- Show native WebMCP discovery before the guided workflow.
- Capture clear pointer movement and wait for each receipt to render.
- Record voice cleanly; do not use copyrighted music.
- Export below 2:50 to leave safety margin under the three-minute limit.
- Review the final YouTube upload in a logged-out window before adding its URL.
